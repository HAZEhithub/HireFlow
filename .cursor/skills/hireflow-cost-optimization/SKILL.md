# HireFlow Cost Optimization

1. Measure the current workload, usage drivers and required service levels.
2. Separate fixed, variable, one-time and hidden costs.
3. Compare current provider pricing/free limits from official sources and record access dates.
4. Prefer deterministic/local/open-source processing when it is operationally viable.
5. Apply caching, batching, deduplication, bounded concurrency, quotas and graceful degradation.
6. Model costs at stated user/traffic volumes; state assumptions and uncertainty.
7. Evaluate privacy, license, support, retention and exit cost, not price alone.
8. Do not provision paid resources or enable billing without approval.

Do not call a service free without stating relevant quotas, inactivity behavior, restrictions and upgrade triggers.
