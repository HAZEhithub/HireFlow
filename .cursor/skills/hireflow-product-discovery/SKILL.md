# HireFlow Product Discovery

## When to use
Use before adding major product areas, changing positioning, creating roadmap priorities or evaluating competitors.

## Workflow
1. Inspect existing HireFlow docs and implementation; identify what already exists.
2. Define target personas and concrete pain points.
3. Research relevant competitor workflows, current features and pricing using official sources first; corroborate with independent feedback.
4. Capture source URLs and access dates; distinguish facts, anecdotes and hypotheses.
5. Map current/future journeys and failure states.
6. Score opportunities by user impact, confidence, effort, differentiation, privacy risk, ongoing cost and dependencies.
7. Recommend a phased plan with acceptance criteria and validation experiments.
8. Record assumptions and unresolved questions; do not invent evidence.

## Deliverable
A concise decision memo with findings, source register, recommendation, non-goals, phase, risks and metrics.
