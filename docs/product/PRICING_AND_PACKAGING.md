# Pricing and packaging

**Date:** 2026-11-21  
**Status:** Approved commercial direction. **Billing is not live.**

## Approved India price

| Plan | List price | Billing |
|------|------------|---------|
| Free | **₹0** | Live as the only grantable plan |
| Pro | **₹199/month + applicable GST** | Not collecting payment |

An **indicative** total at a **common 18% GST hypothesis** is **₹234.82/month**. 18% is **not** a legal determination of HireFlow’s GST rate, SAC, or place of supply.

Do not silently change ₹199.

## What is free (now)

Account, session, profile, settings, empty Jobs / Resume Studio / Applications, in-app inbox, usage meter, JSON export, account deletion.

## What is free (when those modules ship)

Job search on permitted sources, saved jobs (unlimited), URL import with an abuse cap, explainable matching, two resume versions, application tracker, in-app follow-ups, data export.

## What Pro is intended to add (planned — do not advertise as live)

Job-specific tailoring, cover-letter drafts (user-approved), higher AI assist cap (50/month, not unlimited), unlimited resume versions, application analytics (small-n), evidence map, company brief, search strategy coach, skill graph.

## Checkout

No payment gateway, no cards, no recurring charges, no automatic upgrade. Pricing page CTAs go to Start Free.
